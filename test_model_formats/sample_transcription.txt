The United Methodist Church, UMC, is a mainline Protestant Methodist denomination. In the 19th century its main predecessor was a leader in Evangelicalism. Founded in 1968 by the Union of the Methodist Church U.S.A. and the Evangelical United Brethren Church. The UMC traces its roots back to the revival movement of John and Charles Wesley in England as well as the Great Awakening in the United States. As such, the Church's theological orientation is decidedly Wesleyan. It embraces both liturgical and evangelical elements.
